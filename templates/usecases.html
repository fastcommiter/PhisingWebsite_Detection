Phishing Scenarios & Data Breaches

Real-world examples of how phishing impacts individuals and organizations.

  • Use Case #1: John Podesta’s Email Breach

    The 2016 hack of John Podesta’s Gmail account remains a classic spear-phishing example. Attackers disguised as Google sent a security alert. When the compromised link was clicked, hackers gained full access.

  • Use Case #2: BenefitMall Payroll Solutions

    BenefitMall suffered a breach where employee credentials were stolen through phishing. This exposed sensitive info like Bank Accounts and SSNs of over 200,000 businesses.
